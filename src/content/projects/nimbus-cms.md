## Overview

A digital publisher was stuck on an ageing CMS that slowed editors down and buckled during traffic spikes. We split content management from delivery.

## Approach

- Laravel-based headless API with a modern editorial UI.
- Incremental static regeneration for article pages behind a CDN.
- Scripted, repeatable migration of 40k articles with redirects preserved.

## Outcome

Faster pages for readers, a happier editorial team and a far smaller infrastructure bill.
