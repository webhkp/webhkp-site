## Overview

Pathfinder Learn turns scattered tutorials into structured paths, so learners always know what to study next and why it matters.

## Approach

- Paths built from bite-sized lessons, coding exercises and checkpoints.
- Progress tracking and spaced-repetition review.
- Tight integration with BigBox network articles as reference material.

## Outcome

A clearer, more motivating way to learn software engineering end to end.
